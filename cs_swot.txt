  Overall: positive (medium confidence)
  The career outlook for computer science professionals in Sri Lanka appears promising by 2031, driven by increased demand for skilled IT roles and enhanced government support for the sector. While competition may be challenging, especially for entry-level positions, prospective graduates can find numerous opportunities in software engineering, cybersecurity, and related fields. However, economic factors and automation may pose risks that need to be navigated.
  Strengths:
    - High demand for IT roles in Sri Lanka indicates a robust entry point for graduates in 2031. [E001, E003, E014]
    - Growing emphasis on practical skills and relevant certifications among employers. [E006, E012]
  Weaknesses:
    - High competition among graduates for common roles in software engineering and cybersecurity. [E006, E012]
    - Limited internship opportunities available, which may hinder experiential learning for new entrants. [E009, E013]
  Opportunities:
    - Government and private-sector investments in IT education and infrastructure could enhance career prospects. [E002, E011, E014]
    - Increasing remote job opportunities for skilled IT professionals may expand market reach beyond local boundaries. [E003, E004]
  Threats:
    - Economic challenges and fluctuations may affect overall job stability in the IT sector. [E014]
