# G.C.E. (O/L) Requirements and Practical/Aptitude Test Mappings

_Source: UGC Admission to Undergraduate Courses of the Universities in Sri Lanka, Academic Year 2024/2025._

## G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) Examination Requirements

Every candidate who has listed any of the following courses of study among his/her preferences must submit a duly certified copy of the original G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) Examination certificate showing that the required passes have been obtained.

If the required certificate is not submitted with the printed application, the candidate will not be considered for the relevant course of study.

The required G.C.E. (O/L) qualifications must have been fulfilled by the deadline for submission of applications for university admission.

The candidate should be a minimum of **4 ft 10 in** in height and should not have any kind of deformity in order to be eligible for the course of study in Nursing.

## Requirement to Pass the Practical / Aptitude Test

Every candidate who has listed any of the following courses of study among his/her preferences must pass the practical/aptitude test conducted by the respective university, campus, or institute.

The relevant institution will publish a press notice calling for applications for the practical/aptitude test. Candidates must apply directly to the respective institution.

Passing the practical/aptitude test alone is not sufficient. The candidate must also satisfy the applicable cut-off mark for the course of study.
